For the record

I figured I’d write this down, mostly because I can’t stand the inefficiency of this vague silence anymore. It’s a waste of time. Let’s just call it what it is. It's pretty obvious that you’ve distanced yourself. I’m not asking for a reason, and I’m definitely not asking you to change your mind.

I'm just acknowledging that you did exactly what you said you would never do. You made very specific promises about being there and not leaving when things got difficult. It’s almost impressive how predictable it turned out to be.

To be direct, I made the mistake of actually starting to consider a future that included you. For a guy like me, who hasn't cared about a future for a very long time, that was a pretty massive lapse in judgment. I let my guard down, and this is the result. Losing that hasn't made me angry; anger takes too much energy. It’s just left me exhausted. It’s that deep, bone-tired feeling you get when you realize you’ve been running a race that was rigged from the start.

Since you’ve been gone, my mental state has regressed. I’ve fallen back into the old bad habits and patterns, because they’re the only things that seem to help. My mental health is back to its original state. If we were to talk now, you’d find me intolerable. It's funny because this is how I used to avoid people, and how I'd see through their BS.

The night I realized you were never coming back, I sat there for hours. Crying my eyes out, until I eventually just felt numb. It was just when I realized that every word you said about being proud of me or wanting to carry my pain, it was just a performance. Maybe you believed it while you were saying it, but that doesn't change the fact that it was hollow. It wasn't strong enough to survive a little bit of reality. That’s the problem with people who promise to save you. They always forget that saving someone is actually hard work.

My memory is becoming pretty unreliable lately. Probably due to the burnout and the constant state of stress, most days feel like a blur. But I still remember the sensation of thinking I was actually cared for. It was a nice hallucination while it lasted. Even if it was fake, the memory of that feeling remains, reminding me that I was stupid enough to believe in something that didn't exist. It just makes the current hollowness feel more logical.

I don't have the energy for a conflict or some big dramatic confrontation. I’m just tired. I’m tired of the noise, the empty reassurances, and the fake warmth people offer just to make themselves feel better before they get bored and move on. I’m returning to the state I was in before I knew you. It’s a bitter, cold place. It's where I don't have to worry about someone leaving when I'm already by myself. It’s a state I’m much more familiar with anyway.

I don't think we have anything left to say.
